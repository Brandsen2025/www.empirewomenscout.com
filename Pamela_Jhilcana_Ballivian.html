Global Impact Analysis

Pamela Ballivian represents the new generation of Bolivian talent capable of competing at the continental level. A versatile attacker who can operate on the wings or as a second striker, she combines "Yungueña" endurance with sharp technical dribbling. Her performances in the Copa Libertadores Femenina for Club Always Ready have showcased her ability to break defensive lines and create chaos in the final third.

Scout Verdict: "The Disruptor." Ballivian possesses a low center of gravity and high work rate, typical of the modern pressing forward. While she needs to refine her final decision-making, her raw ability to carry the ball into dangerous zones makes her a high-value asset for South American leagues and a potential target for developing European sides.
