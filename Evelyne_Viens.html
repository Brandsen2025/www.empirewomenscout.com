🌍 Career Journey — From Québec to Serie A

Unconventional path: College → NWSL → Europe → Olympic champion.

1997-2016 — L'Ancienne-Lorette, Québec
Born in Québec City suburb. Developed in local youth football. Quebec U17/U18 MVP and Golden Boot winner. CEGEP All-Star. Canadian youth international pathway.
2016-2019 — University of South Florida
Collegiate dominance. 73 career goals (USF record). 3-time All-American. AAC all-time leading scorer. 2019 Senior Class Award finalist. Led USF to first AAC regular season title.
2020-2021 — NJ/NY Gotham FC
NWSL debut. 5th overall draft pick. First professional goal in Challenge Cup semifinal. Limited minutes but gained professional experience. Tokyo 2020 Olympics call-up (gold medal).
2021 — Paris FC (Loan)
European breakthrough. 11 goals in 14 matches in Division 1 Féminine. Proved ability to score in top-tier European league. Returned to NWSL after loan.
2022-2023 — Kristianstads DFF
Canadian record. 21 goals in 26 Damallsvenskan matches (Canadian record). 2nd in league scoring. Hat tricks vs AIK and Djurgårdens. Established as European elite.
2023-Present — AS Roma
Serie A arrival. Joined Italian champions. 13 goals in 2024-25. Top-5 league scorer. UWCL participant. Becoming face of Canadian strikers in Europe.
The Unconventional Path: Viens' journey is unusual for a Canadian striker — most take the NWSL route exclusively. Her decision to seek European opportunities (Paris FC loan, Sweden, Italy) demonstrates ambition and adaptability. The Olympic gold medal (2020) validates her as Canada's clutch performer. Her 21-goal Swedish season proved she could compete with Europe's best. Now at Roma, she has found the perfect platform — a top club in a growing league with UWCL exposure.
